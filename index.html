Our Love Story

2013

First Meeting

“It was not much but…”.Two kids, same classroom. Maybe just a glance, a shared class, or a borrowed pencil. No sparks yet, just fate’s quiet setup. Group projects, lunch tables, school events. Inside jokes start forming. Compete through academics and life pulls you apart, but you stay in touch. Distance confirms the connection is real.

2021

First Date

Bold move. I didn’t just date her. I dated her world. Her family watched, judged (kindly), and ate buffet foods. I passed. Our first real “us” moment was in Xean Winghouse. One sip of juice & one bite of chicken wing.

2024

First Plan together

Not just dreams anymore. Real plans and big step. Conversations turned into decisions. We chose our first house and lot, imagining where the couch would go, what color the walls would be, and the life we’d build inside it. It wasn’t just about property it was about choosing each other, again, but this time for the future.

2026

The Proposal

On a quiet, romantic night at Blackbird Makati. Soft lights, good food, and the kind of conversation that feels like home. And pause a moment, nervous but certain. Everything led here. She said a big YES.
