Our Love Story

2009

First Meeting

“It was not much but…”.Two kids, same classroom. Maybe just a glance, a shared class, or a borrowed 1/4 sheet of paper. No sparks yet, just fate’s quiet setup. Group projects, lunch tables, school events. Inside jokes start forming. Compete through academics and life pulls us apart, but we stay in touch occasionally during birthday and reunions. Distance confirms that the connection for us is real and possible.

2021

First Reconnection

Through Facebook, we found our way back to each other with a simple “Kamusta ang Japan?” What started as a small message turned into long nights of endless conversations and daily updates we both looked forward to. Little by little, we grew closer again until one day, I asked for permission for us to become officially boyfriend and girlfriend, and from that moment, our story began once more.

2022

First Date

Bold move. I didn’t just date her. I dated her world. Her family watched, judged kindly, and we ate buffet foods.Somehow I passed. Our first real “us” moment was in Flame Stone Restobar. One sip of smoothies & one bite of chicken wings.

2024

First Plan together

Not just dreams anymore. Real plans and big step. Conversations turned into decisions. We chose our first house and lot, imagining where the couch would go, what color the walls would be, and the life we’d build inside it. It wasn’t just about property it was about choosing each other, again, but this time for the future.

2026

The Proposal

On a quiet, romantic night at Blackbird Makati. Soft lights, good food, and the kind of conversation that feels like home. And pause a moment, nervous but certain. Everything led here. She said a big YES.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the dress code?

Guests are encouraged to wear formal attire in shades of GREEN, CHAMPAGNE, BEIGE, or NEUTRAL TONES. Please refer to the attire guide for full details.

Can I bring a plus one?

We are really sorry, but we’re keeping the guest list very tight and CANNOT ACCOMMODATE EXTRA PEOPLE. We hope you can still make it, We'd love to have you there.

Do you prefer cash gifts?

YES, since we are not based locally, we kindly request monetary gifts for convenience. Your presence is truly the most meaningful gift, but if you wish to give, a cash gift would be sincerely appreciated.

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive 30 MINUTES before the ceremony (3:30 PM).

Is there parking available?

YES, complimentary parking will be provided at the entrance of L.D Ignacio.

Will the ceremony be indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony will be held OUTDOORS at 4:00 PM, when the heat is more manageable. Guests may still bring small essentials like a fan or shawl for added comfort.

Who can guests contact for questions on the wedding day?

For any assistance on the wedding day, please feel free to contact our WEDDING COORDINATOR. We kindly ask to avoid reaching out to the couple directly so we can fully enjoy this special moment with you.

Are there assigned seating arrangements?

YES for entourage members, but NO for general guests. Entourage members will have designated seats, while guests are free to choose their seats upon arrival.

Are children welcome?

As much as we adore your little ones, we kindly request that our celebration remain an ADULTS-ONLY event, with the exception on our bearers. Thank you for your understanding.

What food options are available?

We prepare NO PORK, VEGETARIAN, and NO RESTRICTION food options. Please specify in your RSVP.
